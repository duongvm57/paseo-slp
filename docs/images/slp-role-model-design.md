# Governance Constellation

This visual language treats authority as a constellation rather than a chain.
The central field is a quiet midnight grid: a measured space where each role
has a distinct orbit and no card can be mistaken for a rung in a hierarchy.
Human authority is a warm source above the system; it establishes intent and
decision boundaries without becoming a management layer.

Supervisor and Lead use separate colour families and sit on an equal horizon.
The Supervisor's sea-glass green expresses observation, process quality and
signals; the Lead's violet expresses project authority, integration and
verdict. Their dotted connection is deliberately lateral and labelled as
observation, so the diagram makes the separation of governance and execution
visible before a reader reaches the explanatory text.

The Peer cluster is introduced by one large `PEER(S)` field mark and a single
cyan divider. Engineer, Architect, Reviewer and Scout sit beneath it as
assignment dispositions rather than peer role variants. Its connections
distinguish delegation from the upward return of evidence, challenge and
blocked work. The role names are sparse and practical; the composition carries
the fuller meaning through direction, spacing and weight.

Fine grid lines, restrained halos and carefully weighted arrows give the image
the feel of a control-room instrument rather than decorative product art. The
work is deliberately precise: every gap preserves hierarchy-free reading,
every colour is assigned one meaning, and every line explains a contract edge
that readers need in order to operate the policy safely.
